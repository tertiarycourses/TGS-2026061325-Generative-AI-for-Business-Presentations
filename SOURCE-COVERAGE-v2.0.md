## Coverage in artifacts

- **Slides:** visual principles, audience/decision framing, story structures, AI tool-fit, human-in-the-loop workflow, collateral choices, visual evidence, delivery, Q&A, negotiation, outcome review, do/don't comparisons and applied examples.
- **Learner Guide:** detailed procedures, tool-specific practice routes, privacy/source/accessibility checks, prompts, activity instructions and references.
- **Activities:** eight connected workplace applications spanning A1–A4 and producing editable artifacts.
- **Assessment:** four open-ended knowledge questions covering K1–K3 and a four-task Case Study covering A1–A4, preserving the original instrument structure.
