## About the course

The deck teaches presentation principles, business storytelling, visual evidence, delivery, Q&A, negotiation, review, and responsible AI-assisted production. Eight connected activities follow the Meridian Foods ReadyWise business scenario. Detailed software procedures are kept in the Learner Guide and activity packs rather than on the slides.

## Learning outcomes

- LO1: Establish business presentation objectives and select presentation modes according to organisational context and stakeholder needs.
- LO2: Determine presentation deliverables and design targeted presentation collaterals using suitable generative AI tools.
- LO3: Deliver appropriate business messages using suitable presentation modes and techniques to engage, persuade and handle discussion.
- LO4: Review presentation outcomes and recommend improvements using evidence, feedback and continuous-improvement methods.

## Topics covered

1. Context, Objectives and Modes
2. AI-Assisted Story and Collateral Design
3. Engage, Persuade and Handle Discussion
4. Review Outcomes and Improve
