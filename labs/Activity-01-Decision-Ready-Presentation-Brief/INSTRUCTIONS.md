# Activity 01 — Decision-Ready Presentation Brief

**Course:** Generative AI for Business Presentations (TGS-2026061325)  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Tools:** PowerPoint + Claude  
**Output:** A one-page presentation brief containing context, audience, desired action, evidence, constraints, mode and success measure.

## Objective

Frame a presentation objective, audience action, evidence need and delivery mode.

## Scenario

Meridian Foods Singapore is considering ReadyWise, an AI-assisted meal-planning service bundled with a new ready-meal range. Leadership has reserved 20 minutes at the next investment committee meeting.
The CFO worries about margin, the Data Protection Officer worries about customer-data use, and the Sales Director wants retailer evidence. Your team must turn this broad situation into a presentation brief before generating slides.

## Detailed instructions

1. **Open the brief template.** Record the business situation in two sentences: what is happening and why a decision is needed now.

2. **Map the audience.** For the Sponsor, CFO, DPO and Sales Director, record decision role, existing knowledge, likely concern and evidence need.

3. **Write the audience action.** Complete: 'At the end of the presentation, the committee will…' Use one observable decision or action.

4. **Write the objective.** Combine audience, action, business outcome and timing in one sentence. Remove vague verbs such as know, learn or appreciate.

5. **Define proof.** List the three items of evidence the audience needs. Mark any assumption or data gap that must be verified.

6. **Record constraints.** Specify time, confidentiality, brand, accessibility, device, room and file-format limits.

7. **Choose the mode.** Score live, virtual, hybrid and self-guided against interaction, complexity, access and follow-up. Select one and explain the trade-off.

8. **Set success evidence.** Name what you will observe immediately after the presentation and what follow-through will be checked later.

9. **Peer-check.** Swap briefs. The reviewer highlights anything ambiguous, unsupported or inconsistent; revise before submission.

## Suggested timing

- 5 minutes — Read the case and assign stakeholder roles
- 8 minutes — Map audience knowledge, power and concerns
- 10 minutes — Write the objective and audience action
- 10 minutes — Define evidence, constraints and success measures
- 7 minutes — Select and defend the mode
- 5 minutes — Peer-check and revise

## Submission

Submit the completed activity artefact, the checklist and the editable presentation file. Record all AI tools, prompts, sources and human changes used.

## Debrief

The brief fits on one page and makes audience, action, evidence, constraints, mode and success measure explicit.
