# Activity 05 — Evidence-to-Visual Upgrade with Napkin

**Course:** Generative AI for Business Presentations (TGS-2026061325)  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Tools:** Napkin + PowerPoint or Canva  
**Output:** Three rebuilt visual slides: an evidence chart, a process map and a decision comparison.

## Objective

Replace weak text and decorative graphics with decision-useful visual evidence.

## Scenario

Meridian's AI draft contains a dense financial slide, a generic handshake photo and a six-step implementation paragraph. The committee needs to understand the economics, risk gates and ownership in under two minutes.
Use Napkin to explore a process visual, and PowerPoint or Canva to rebuild the economics and implementation slides as editable evidence-led visuals.

## Detailed instructions

1. **State the claim.** For each weak slide, write the one message the audience must understand. If there are two messages, split the slide.

2. **Identify the relationship.** Label it comparison, trend, composition, distribution, process, hierarchy, geography or exact values.

3. **Select the form.** Choose the simplest chart, table or diagram that reveals that relationship. Explain why an alternative would be weaker.

4. **Use Napkin for the process.** Paste a public-safe six-line process description into Napkin. Generate alternatives, then select and simplify one.

5. **Rebuild as editable.** Recreate or clean the chosen visual in PowerPoint or Canva so labels, colours and data remain controllable.

6. **Write the action title.** State the conclusion, not the chart type. Add a short subtitle only if scope or time period matters.

7. **Direct attention.** Use one accent colour, direct labels and a short annotation on the decision-relevant value. Remove legends where direct labels work.

8. **Add integrity cues.** Show source, date, base, unit and uncertainty or assumption. Start bar-chart axes at zero unless a justified exception is explicit.

9. **Run the distance check.** View at 25% zoom or from the back of the room. Confirm the title, main pattern and implication remain legible.

## Suggested timing

- 5 minutes — Identify the decision claim on three weak slides
- 8 minutes — Select the right visual relationship
- 10 minutes — Explore one Napkin process visual
- 12 minutes — Rebuild in PowerPoint or Canva
- 7 minutes — Add titles, sources and qualifiers
- 3 minutes — Distance-check

## Submission

Submit the completed activity artefact, the checklist and the editable presentation file. Record all AI tools, prompts, sources and human changes used.

## Debrief

Three weak slides are replaced with an evidence chart, a process visual and a decision table, each with an action title and source note.
