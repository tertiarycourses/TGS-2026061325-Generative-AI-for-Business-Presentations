# Activity 07 — Negotiation Deck Challenge

**Course:** Generative AI for Business Presentations (TGS-2026061325)  
**Duration:** 40 minutes  
**Tools:** Claude + PowerPoint  
**Output:** A four-slide negotiation pack and documented conditional agreement.

## Objective

Support a business negotiation with clear interests, options, trade-offs and decision boundaries.

## Scenario

The committee supports ReadyWise in principle but disagrees on scale. Build a four-slide negotiation pack that makes interests, options, trade-offs and authority boundaries visible, then negotiate a conditional decision.

## Detailed instructions

1. **Separate positions from interests.** Each role writes its stated position and the underlying need it protects. Share interests before arguing solutions.

2. **Define criteria.** Agree how packages will be judged: learning value, downside risk, privacy control, retailer readiness, cost and timing.

3. **Mark authority boundaries.** Each role states preferred, acceptable and not-authorised ranges for spend, retailer count and review gates.

4. **Build three packages.** Create Conservative, Recommended and Accelerated options. Show variables in aligned rows so trade-offs are visible.

5. **Create the four-slide pack.** Use shared objective, interests/criteria, option comparison and conditional-decision record.

6. **Open with common ground.** The Sponsor restates the shared objective and confirms the decision criteria before discussing packages.

7. **Trade conditionally.** Use if–then language: 'If we add two retailers, then we need…'. Never concede one variable without linking it to another.

8. **Test agreement.** Ask each role to state what they believe has been agreed and what remains unresolved.

9. **Record the decision.** Capture package, conditions, owners, dates, review gates and escalation path on the final slide.

## Suggested timing

- 5 minutes — Assign roles and list underlying interests
- 8 minutes — Define shared criteria and boundaries
- 10 minutes — Build three option packages
- 10 minutes — Negotiate using the four-slide pack
- 5 minutes — Record conditional agreement
- 2 minutes — Reflect

## Submission

Submit the completed activity artefact, the checklist and the editable presentation file. Record all AI tools, prompts, sources and human changes used.

## Debrief

The final slide records the agreed package, conditions, owners, dates and unresolved questions.
