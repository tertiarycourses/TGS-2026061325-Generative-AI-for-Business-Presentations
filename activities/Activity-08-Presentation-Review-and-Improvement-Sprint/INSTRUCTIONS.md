# Activity 08 — Presentation Review and Improvement Sprint

**Course:** Generative AI for Business Presentations (TGS-2026061325)  
**Duration:** 50 minutes  
**Tools:** PowerPoint + Canva + Claude or Microsoft 365 Copilot  
**Output:** An outcome scorecard, improvement backlog, controlled version record and three revised slides.

## Objective

Evaluate presentation outcomes and prioritise evidence-based improvements.

## Scenario

Meridian's pilot was approved conditionally. The audience understood the commercial case but remained unclear about personal-data boundaries. The CFO requested a downside view, and the final decision slide did not name the DPO deliverable.
Use peer evidence, meeting notes and an AI critique from Claude or Copilot to diagnose the deck, then create a controlled improvement backlog and revised master.

## Detailed instructions

1. **Return to the brief.** Copy the original audience action and success evidence from Activity 1. Do not begin with aesthetic opinions.

2. **Collect evidence.** List the decision, conditions, questions, misunderstood points, follow-up commitments and any available engagement or viewing data.

3. **Score five dimensions.** Rate storyline, evidence, design, delivery and interaction 1–5. Cite one observed fact for each score.

4. **Request AI critique.** Give Claude or Copilot the audience, objective and a redacted deck or outline. Ask it to review against the five dimensions and label uncertainty.

5. **Triangulate.** Keep AI suggestions only when supported by outcome evidence, a course principle or reviewer consensus. Reject unsupported stylistic preference.

6. **Diagnose root causes.** Use 'because' until the issue is actionable: e.g., privacy remained unclear because the boundary was described in notes but not visualised.

7. **Prioritise changes.** Score impact on decision quality and effort. Select three high-impact revisions and place the rest in the backlog.

8. **Assign ownership.** For every change, add owner, due date, source needed, acceptance criterion and reviewer.

9. **Revise and retest.** Update three slides in the master deck, repeat the title-only and distance checks, and rehearse the critical minute.

10. **Control the version.** Record file name, version, date, author, reviewer, changes and approval status. Archive—not overwrite—the previous approved version.

## Suggested timing

- 5 minutes — Restate objective and collect outcome evidence
- 10 minutes — Score five dimensions
- 8 minutes — Use AI critique as a second opinion
- 10 minutes — Diagnose root causes
- 10 minutes — Prioritise and assign revisions
- 5 minutes — Revise three slides
- 2 minutes — Record the version

## Submission

Submit the completed activity artefact, the checklist and the editable presentation file. Record all AI tools, prompts, sources and human changes used.

## Debrief

The team produces an evidence-based scorecard, prioritised backlog, version record and three revised slides.
