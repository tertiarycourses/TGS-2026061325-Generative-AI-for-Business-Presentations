# Activity 06 — Delivery and Q&A Rehearsal

**Course:** Generative AI for Business Presentations (TGS-2026061325)  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Tools:** PowerPoint Presenter View + Microsoft 365 Copilot  
**Output:** A timed rehearsal, completed peer scorecard and revised speaker notes.

## Objective

Deliver a concise recommendation and respond to stakeholder questions with evidence.

## Scenario

Rehearse a six-minute version of Meridian's ReadyWise recommendation followed by four minutes of stakeholder questions. Use PowerPoint Presenter View and Copilot-generated speaker-note options, but deliver in your own words.

## Detailed instructions

1. **Assign roles.** Choose Presenter, CFO and DPO or Sales Director. Give the questioners the role briefs but do not show their questions to the presenter.

2. **Prepare the route.** The presenter writes a 30-second opening, three transition lines and a 30-second decision close. Use notes as prompts, not a script.

3. **Set the room.** Open the deck in Presenter View, test display and timer, silence notifications and confirm a backup PDF.

4. **Deliver six minutes.** Lead with the decision, guide the audience through evidence and risks, and finish on the requested action.

5. **Ask two questions.** Each stakeholder asks one objection and one follow-up. The presenter uses Acknowledge–Clarify–Answer–Confirm.

6. **Score separately.** Peers score clarity, credibility, connection and control using evidence from what they observed.

7. **Debrief specifically.** Each peer gives one keep and one change, tied to a moment in the rehearsal rather than a general preference.

8. **Revise notes.** Use Copilot or Claude to propose concise note alternatives for the weak moment, then rewrite them in the presenter's own voice.

9. **Repeat the critical minute.** Redeliver the opening, difficult answer or close and compare it against the original score.

## Suggested timing

- 5 minutes — Assign roles and set a timer
- 8 minutes — Prepare opening, transitions and close
- 10 minutes — Deliver the six-minute presentation
- 10 minutes — Run four minutes of Q&A
- 7 minutes — Score and discuss
- 5 minutes — Revise speaker notes

## Submission

Submit the completed activity artefact, the checklist and the editable presentation file. Record all AI tools, prompts, sources and human changes used.

## Debrief

The presenter completes the opening, recommendation, evidence, two questions and decision close within ten minutes.
