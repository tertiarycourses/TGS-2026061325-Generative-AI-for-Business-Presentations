# Activity 03 — Executive Story Spine with Claude or Copilot

**Course:** Generative AI for Business Presentations (TGS-2026061325)  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Tools:** Claude or Microsoft 365 Copilot  
**Output:** A nine-slide, conclusion-first story map with action titles, source notes and an appendix plan.

## Objective

Turn a presentation brief into a conclusion-first business storyline and slide map.

## Scenario

The committee's slot is only 20 minutes. Meridian has enough material for 30 slides, but leadership wants the recommendation first and will question margin, data use and retailer readiness.
Use Claude or Microsoft 365 Copilot to generate alternatives, then select and edit a nine-slide storyline that answers the decision without hiding the risks.

## Detailed instructions

1. **Prepare inputs.** Use only approved facts from the brief. State audience, decision, time limit, evidence, objections and tone.

2. **Prompt for alternatives.** Ask Claude or Copilot for three distinct SCQA story spines. Require it to mark assumptions and missing evidence.

3. **Challenge the answer.** Ask which claim is weakest, what the CFO and DPO could contest, and what evidence would resolve each objection.

4. **Select one spine.** Choose the option that reaches the decision fastest without hiding material risks. Write Situation, Complication, Question and Answer in one line each.

5. **Map nine slides.** Create a row for each slide: action title, audience question, evidence, intended visual and speaker purpose.

6. **Write action titles.** Use complete sentences that state the takeaway. Replace labels such as 'Market Opportunity' with the conclusion the evidence supports.

7. **Plan the appendix.** Move detailed calculations, methods and secondary evidence out of the main narrative while preserving access for Q&A.

8. **Trace every claim.** Add a source or 'assumption to validate' beside every quantitative and factual statement.

9. **Run the title-only test.** Read only the nine slide titles aloud. Revise until the recommendation, proof, risk and action form a coherent argument.

## Suggested timing

- 5 minutes — Prepare the safe prompt and source summary
- 10 minutes — Generate three story alternatives
- 10 minutes — Test logic and objections
- 12 minutes — Write the nine-slide map
- 5 minutes — Trace claims to evidence
- 3 minutes — Read the title-only story

## Submission

Submit the completed activity artefact, the checklist and the editable presentation file. Record all AI tools, prompts, sources and human changes used.

## Debrief

The story map has a visible SCQA spine, recommendation-first opening, action titles and source notes for every claim.
