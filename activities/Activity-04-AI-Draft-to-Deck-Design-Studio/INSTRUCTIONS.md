# Activity 04 — AI Draft-to-Deck Design Studio

**Course:** Generative AI for Business Presentations (TGS-2026061325)  
**Duration:** 50 minutes  
**Tools:** PowerPoint/Copilot · Canva · Gamma · Adobe Express · Presenton · Manus · QuillBot · Prezent  
**Output:** An editable nine-slide deck draft plus a six-gate AI-output audit.

## Objective

Generate and refine an editable, branded and accessible presentation draft.

## Scenario

The story map is approved. Each team will produce one editable draft in PowerPoint/Copilot, Canva, Gamma, Adobe Express, Presenton, Manus, QuillBot or Prezent, then bring the strongest elements into a controlled PowerPoint master.
The committee will see the deck on a large screen and receive a self-guided PDF afterwards. All numbers must remain traceable to Meridian's source pack.

## Detailed instructions

1. **Set the production rules.** Specify 16:9, approved colours and fonts, audience, nine-slide map, one message per slide, editable objects, source notes and no invented facts.

2. **Generate two directions.** Create two draft visual systems or layouts. Treat them as alternatives, not finished decks.

3. **Freeze the evidence.** Replace AI-generated figures with verified values from the source record. Delete claims that cannot be supported.

4. **Audit the logic.** Check that the recommendation, evidence, risks and next action remain aligned with the approved story map.

5. **Audit the design.** Check hierarchy, density, contrast, typography, consistency, whitespace and whether the visual matches the relationship.

6. **Audit accessibility.** Add meaningful titles, readable type, adequate contrast, alt text and non-colour cues. Avoid text embedded only in images.

7. **Rebuild priority slides.** Create purpose-built title, decision, evidence, risk and close slides in the controlled PowerPoint master.

8. **Test hand-off.** Export PPTX and PDF. Reopen them on another machine or viewer; check fonts, layout, animations, notes and links.

9. **Record the trail.** Save prompts, tool names, dates, source list, reviewer, unresolved issues and final approval status.

## Suggested timing

- 5 minutes — Choose the generation workflow
- 10 minutes — Write and run the production prompt
- 12 minutes — Audit the first draft
- 15 minutes — Rebuild priority slides
- 5 minutes — Test export
- 3 minutes — Record the review trail

## Submission

Submit the completed activity artefact, the checklist and the editable presentation file. Record all AI tools, prompts, sources and human changes used.

## Debrief

The draft is editable and passes the six gates: logic, evidence, design, brand, accessibility and export.
